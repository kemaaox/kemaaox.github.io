Panelist in a PBS North Carolina discussion accompanying the Independent Lens preview of the documentary *Coded Bias*, about bias in artificial intelligence and algorithmic decision-making. Fellow panelists were Dr. Sarra Alqahtani (Wake Forest University) and Dr. Hai "Helen" Li (Duke University), and the moderator was Shannon Henry of PBS North Carolina.
