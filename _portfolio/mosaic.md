Traditional query languages assume users know exactly what they are looking for and how the data is structured. Many tasks on the Semantic Web, the "structured web", are instead *discovery-oriented*: users want to explore, find unexpected connections, or assemble combinations of items that together satisfy their needs.

**MOSAIC** developed advanced querying paradigms and techniques for such tasks, including:

* **Keyword queries on RDF databases:** interpreting and disambiguating keyword queries (deep segmentation and context-sensitive interpretation)
* **Structure discovery and exploratory queries:** agglomerative query models for discovery in Linked Data and constrained subgraph extraction over large knowledge graphs
* **Package and skyline queries:** finding not just the best items but the best *combinations* (packages) of items. SkyPackage received the Best Paper Award at JIST 2012.
* **Scalable personalized semantic search** over large RDF data
