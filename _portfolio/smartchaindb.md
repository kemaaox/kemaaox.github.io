Blockchains promise to mediate transactions between parties that do not trust each other without a central authority. Today, however, most non-trivial blockchain transactions are written as user-programmed smart contracts, which are hard to get right, hard to optimize, and opaque to the platform executing them.

The **SmartChainDB** project builds middleware for *smart digital marketplaces* on top of an open-source blockchain database platform (BigchainDB). Instead of arbitrary code, transactions are described declaratively, with semantic types that the platform can validate, compose, and optimize. Directions include:

* **Declarative and typed transactions:** transaction primitives and SHACL-based transaction models that make complex marketplace interactions (requests, bids, accepts, returns) robust and efficient
* **Smart assets:** modeling digital assets and their behavior on the blockchain
* **Semantic events:** detecting meaningful events in blockchain transaction streams
* **Privacy:** group signatures that preserve buyer anonymity in decentralized supply-chain marketplaces
* **Applications:** decentralized manufacturing and cloud manufacturing-as-a-service marketplaces
