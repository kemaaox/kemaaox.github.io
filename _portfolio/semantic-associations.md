Many important questions, in national security, bioinformatics, and business intelligence, are not about individual entities but about *how* entities are connected. My doctoral work, in the Semantic Discovery (SemDis) project at the University of Georgia's LSDIS Lab under Prof. Amit Sheth, introduced **semantic associations**: complex relationships between entities in RDF graphs, such as paths or sets of paths, together with ways to query and rank them.

Key contributions include:

* **The ρ-operator and ρ-Queries:** a query operator and query model for discovering semantic associations on the Semantic Web
* **SemRank:** relevance ranking of complex relationship query results, adjustable between conventional and "surprising" results
* **SPARQ2L:** extending SPARQL with path variables and constraints to support subgraph extraction queries in RDF databases
* **Structure discovery and graph summarization:** disk-based structure discovery queries and graph summaries for estimating subgraph frequencies and the cardinality of RDF graph patterns
