<p class="sec-sub">Domain knowledge and knowledge that persists</p>

AI systems increasingly maintain knowledge that outlives a single interaction. An agent retains what it was told and what it concluded; a robot retains what it believes about its surroundings; a decentralized system maintains state that many parties update. In each case what is retained conditions everything that follows.

Most work on machine-maintained knowledge concerns retrieval: what to bring back, and when. We are interested in validity: what it means for such knowledge to be correct, and how correctness can be maintained as the knowledge changes. This is a natural setting for declarative constraints, and it connects our earlier work on validation over graph-structured data, including [declarative blockchain transactions](/research/smartchaindb/), to current questions in agent and robot autonomy.
