<p class="sec-sub">Domain knowledge in the learning objective</p>

Learning systems typically treat domain knowledge as something applied before training, as feature engineering, or after it, as a check on outputs. We are interested in the space between: how an ontology can inform the objective a model is trained against, particularly in domains where categories are related to one another in ways a flat treatment discards.

Two questions motivate the work. What does structure contribute when labelled data is scarce, and what kinds of explanation become available when the structure that shaped a model is the same structure a domain expert already uses?
