<p class="sec-sub">Domain knowledge at decision time</p>

Some domain requirements are not preferences that can be traded against accuracy. A dietary recommendation that exceeds a clinical limit is not a slightly worse recommendation. We work on systems in which symbolic constraints are enforced where decisions are made, and in which the reasoning that produced a decision is available as its explanation.

Clinical nutrition has been a productive setting for this, because the constraints are genuine, the consequences are real, and acceptability to the person eating the meal is as much a requirement as the numbers.
