Advanced database concepts for graduate students. Topics include the entity-relationship and relational data models and their languages, functional dependencies and normal forms, design and optimization of query languages, security and integrity, concurrency control, transaction processing, and distributed databases.

**Semesters taught:** Spring 2009, Fall 2009, Fall 2010, Spring 2013, Fall 2021, Fall 2022, Fall 2024, Fall 2025, Fall 2026

**Prerequisite:** CSC 316
