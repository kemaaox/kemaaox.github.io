An introduction to database management systems for undergraduates. Topics include the entity-relationship and relational models, relational algebra and SQL, functional dependencies and normalization, database design, security and integrity, concurrency control, and transaction processing.

**Semesters taught:** Spring 2009, Spring 2010, Fall 2010, Spring 2012, Spring 2013, Fall 2022, Fall 2024, Fall 2025

**Prerequisite:** CSC 316 or ECE 309
