A rotating research seminar covering current primary literature in neurosymbolic artificial intelligence.

The course grew out of a graduate special-topics course on knowledge graphs and their role in modern AI systems. That course has now been split in two:

* **CSC 532: Foundations of Neurosymbolic Artificial Intelligence** (new): differentiable formulations of symbolic knowledge, knowledge graph and ontology embeddings, knowledge-grounded retrieval, and the integration patterns that define the field
* **CSC 591/791: Advanced Topics in Neurosymbolic Artificial Intelligence**: this research seminar

**Earlier offerings**
* Spring 2025: CSC 791 *Knowledge Graphs: Foundations, Management and Applications* (Ph.D. students)
* Spring 2026: CSC 791 *Knowledge Graphs & Neurosymbolic AI*
* Spring 2027 (scheduled): CSC 591/791

**Prerequisite:** CSC 540
