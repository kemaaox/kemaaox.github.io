Complex and specialized data structures for building efficient software. Topics include the characteristics of storage media, file organizations, hashing and collision resolution, bit-level structures such as signatures, superimposed coding, and Bloom filters, AVL trees, B-trees and B*-trees, tries, and dynamic hashing.

**Semesters taught:** Fall 2007, Fall 2012, Fall 2021

**Prerequisite:** CSC 316
