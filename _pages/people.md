<div class="sec-head" id="joining-the-lab"><h2>Joining the lab</h2></div>
<p class="sec-sub">Prospective students and collaborators</p>

We work at the boundary between knowledge representation and machine learning. The strongest fit is a student interested in both and willing to be competent in each: comfortable with ontologies, logic, and graph data, and willing to implement and evaluate learning systems rather than only reason about them. See the [Research](/research/) page for our current areas.

<div class="note">
  <p><strong>Prospective PhD students:</strong> apply to the <a href="https://csc.ncsu.edu/academics/graduate/">NC State Computer Science graduate program</a> and name the lab in your statement. Mentioning which of the research areas interests you, and why, is more useful than a general expression of interest.</p>
  <p><strong>Current NC State students:</strong> coursework in knowledge representation, databases, or machine learning is a reasonable entry point. Independent study is often how collaborations start. Email <a href="mailto:kogan@ncsu.edu">kogan@ncsu.edu</a>.</p>
</div>
