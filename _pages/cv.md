**Kemafor Anyanwu Ogan**<br>
Associate Professor, Department of Computer Science, North Carolina State University<br>
2270 Koch Hall, Campus Box 8206, 890 Oval Drive, Raleigh, NC 27695 · [kogan@ncsu.edu](mailto:kogan@ncsu.edu)

Education
======
* **Ph.D. in Computer Science**, University of Georgia, Athens, GA, 2007
  * Dissertation: *Supporting Link Analysis Using Advanced Querying Methods on Semantic Web Databases*
  * Advisor: Prof. Amit Sheth (LSDIS Lab)
* **B.Sc. in Biochemistry**, University of Nigeria, 1989

Academic and professional experience
======
* **Associate Professor**, Department of Computer Science, NC State University, 2014–present
* **Assistant Professor**, Department of Computer Science, NC State University, 2007–2014
* **Founder and Director**, Semantic Computing Research Lab (COUL), NC State University, 2007–present
* **Graduate Research Assistant**, LSDIS Lab (Semantic Discovery project), Department of Computer Science, University of Georgia, until 2007
* **Research Intern**, Advanced Database Group, HP Labs, Palo Alto, CA, Summer 2006
* **Quality Control Chemist**, Serex Inc., New Jersey, 1990–1992

Research interests
======
* Semantic Web and knowledge graph data management
* Big data management and analytics; scalable query processing and optimization
* Blockchain data management and decentralized marketplaces
* Semantics for networked systems and the Internet of Things
* Knowledge graphs and neurosymbolic AI
