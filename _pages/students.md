Prospective students
======
I am always interested in hearing from motivated students who want to work on data management problems at the intersection of the Semantic Web and knowledge graphs, big data systems, and blockchains. If you are a current NC State student, please email me at [kogan@ncsu.edu](mailto:kogan@ncsu.edu) with your CV and a short description of your interests. Prospective Ph.D. students should apply to the [NC State Computer Science graduate program](https://csc.ncsu.edu/academics/graduate/) and mention my name in their application.
