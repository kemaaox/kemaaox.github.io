I am an Associate Professor in the [Department of Computer Science](https://csc.ncsu.edu/) at [North Carolina State University](https://www.ncsu.edu/), where I founded and direct the **Semantic Computing Research Lab (COUL)**. My research is about data: how to model, query, and process richly structured, semantically described data at scale, and how to make the systems that manage it more robust, efficient, and usable.

About me
======
I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the [University of Georgia](https://cs.uga.edu/) in 2007 under the direction of Prof. Amit Sheth, as a member of the Large Scale Distributed Information Systems (LSDIS) Lab, where I worked on the Semantic Discovery (SemDis) project. During my doctoral studies I also interned with the Advanced Database Group at HP Labs in Palo Alto. I joined NC State as an Assistant Professor in 2007 and was promoted to Associate Professor in 2014.

My path into computer science was not a straight line. I earned a B.Sc. in Biochemistry from the University of Nigeria and worked as a quality control chemist, developing and testing enzyme immunoassays at a biotechnology company in New Jersey, before moving into computing.

Research interests
======
* **Semantic Web and knowledge graphs:** RDF data management, SPARQL query processing and optimization, ontological and exploratory querying
* **Big data management and analytics:** scalable graph processing on MapReduce, Hadoop, and Spark; query algebras and dataflow optimization
* **Blockchain data management:** declarative and semantically typed transactions, smart assets, and decentralized marketplaces
* **Semantics for networked systems and the Internet of Things:** semantic models for programmable (software-defined) networks

Research highlights
======
* **Querying for complex relationships.** My early work introduced query models for discovering and ranking *semantic associations*, the complex relationships between entities in RDF graphs: the ρ-operator (SIGMOD Record 2002), ρ-Queries (WWW 2003), SemRank (WWW 2005), and SPARQ2L (WWW 2007).
* **Scaling Semantic Web processing.** With my students I developed the RAPID/RAPID+ systems and the Nested TripleGroup Data Model and Algebra (PVLDB 2011) for processing RDF graph pattern and analytical queries efficiently on MapReduce and other cloud platforms.
* **Advanced query paradigms.** We have studied keyword, structure-discovery, path, and package/skyline queries over RDF. *SkyPackage* received the **Best Paper Award at JIST 2012**.
* **Semantically enabled networks.** In the NSF SERPENT project, in collaboration with RENCI, we modeled software-defined network state semantically so that path and topology problems can be solved with semantic query processing.
* **Blockchains for smart marketplaces.** In the NSF SmartChainDB project we are building a semantically enhanced blockchain platform for decentralized marketplaces, with declarative transaction models, smart assets, and privacy-preserving transactions.

See the [Research](/research/) page for more about these projects and the [Publications](/publications/) page for papers.
