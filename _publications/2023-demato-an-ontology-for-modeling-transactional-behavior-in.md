**Abstract (excerpt):** Blockchains are distributed ledger platforms that were originally envisioned for implementing digital assets and cryptocurrencies such as BITCOIN. Mainstream blockchain platforms such as Ethereum and Hyperledger support both "native" transactional behavior i.e., transferring cryptocurrency assets, as well as, other types of transactional behavior relevant to an emerging class of applications called decentralized applications or DApps.

**Authors:** Sogolsadat Mansouri, Vodelina Samatova, Nodirbek Korchiev, Kemafor Anyanwu
