**Abstract (excerpt):** MapReduce-based data processing platforms offer a promising approach for cost-effective and Web-scale processing of Semantic Web data. However, one major challenge is that this computational paradigm leads to high I/O and communication costs when processing tasks with several join operations typical in SPARQL queries.
