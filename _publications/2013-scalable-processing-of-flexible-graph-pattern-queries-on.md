**Abstract (excerpt):** Flexible exploration of large RDF datasets with unknown relationships can be enabled using 'unbound-property' graph pattern queries. Relational-style processing of such queries using normalized relations results in redundant information in intermediate results due to the repetition of adjoining bound (fixed) properties.
