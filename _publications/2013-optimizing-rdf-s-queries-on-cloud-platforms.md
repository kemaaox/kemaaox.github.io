**Abstract (excerpt):** Scalable processing of Semantic Web queries has become a critical need given the rapid upward trend in availability of Semantic Web data. The MapReduce paradigm is emerging as a platform of choice for large scale data processing and analytics due to its ease of use, cost effectiveness, and potential for unlimited scaling.
