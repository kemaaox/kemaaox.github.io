**Abstract (excerpt):** Life science databases generally consist of multiple heterogeneous datasets that have been integrated using complex ontologies. Querying such databases typically involves complex graph patterns, and evaluating such patterns poses challenges when MapReduce-based platforms are used to scale up processing, translating to long execution workflows with large amount of disk and network I/O costs.
